🧘 Box Breathing Exercise

Calm your nervous system immediately using box breathing:

  1. Inhale slowly through your nose for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold your breath gently for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale fully through your mouth for 6 seconds.
  4. Wait for 2 seconds and repeat 4 times.
